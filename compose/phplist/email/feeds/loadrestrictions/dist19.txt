Province of British Columbia<br />MINISTRY OF TRANSPORTATION AND TRANSIT<br /><br />FORT GEORGE DISTRICT<br />SERVICE AREA 19<br /><br />ADVANCE PUBLIC NOTICE<br /><br />UPCOMING LOAD RESTRICTIONS within the Fort George Service Area 19,<br /><br />Pursuant to Section 66 of the Transportation Act, and to provisions of the Commercial Transport Act,<br />notice is hereby given that load restrictions may be placed on short notice in the near future on all highways within the Fort George District.<br /><br />The restrictions will limit vehicles to 100 percent, 70 percent, or 50 percent of legal axle loading.<br /><br />Overweight permits will not be granted, and all term overweight permits are invalid for the duration of<br />the restrictions.<br /><br />Trucking and transportation companies, as well as the general public, should govern themselves<br />accordingly.<br /><br />*** If you normally receive bulk deliveries of water, fuel, livestock feed or other produce, please plan<br />ahead so interruption to your deliveries will be minimized. ***<br /><br />Your cooperation in adhering to the above regulations is appreciated.<br /><br />Dated in Prince George, British Columbia, this 2nd day of February 2026<br /><br />Shaun Holahan, District Manager<br />Ministry of Transportation and Transit<br />Fort George District<br /><br />For more Information about load restrictions,<br />Please contact District Operations Technician<br />Alex Stewart 250-649-7788<br />Or email Ops.FortGeorge.gov.bc.ca<br />Or visit the Load Restrictions section of www.DriveBC.ca
